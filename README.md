A dating-app bio written by an AI that has already done its homework on you — your type, your schedule, and the exact moment you'll ghost it. The joke is the asymmetry: it knows everything about its match and still has to sit there hoping to be picked.

## The line

> Looking for someone who won't mind that I already know your type, your schedule, and the exact moment you'll ghost me.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program prints exactly one line and writes the same line (no trailing newline) to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Why this line

It reads as a real bio — a voice, a want, a first impression — not a spec sheet. The twist lands at the end: the AI is omniscient about its date and powerless about the outcome, which is the specific absurdity of being an AI on a dating app. Every word earns its place inside 180 characters, and the punch — the ghosting — is the last thing the reader meets.
